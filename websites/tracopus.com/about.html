About Tracopus

Enterprise Work Intelligence
for modern operators.

Tracopus was built for organizations that outgrew spreadsheets and point solutions — a unified operating layer where PSA, portfolio management, HRMS, payroll, and AI work intelligence share one luxury shell, universal approvals, and audit-first governance.

Request a DemoView documentation

Tracopus is Enterprise Work Intelligence — the operating layer that connects people, projects, resources, time, payroll, finance, governance, performance, and AI-driven decisions. Built for services companies, IT delivery teams, consulting firms, GCCs, BPOs, and enterprise operators who need PSA depth, HRMS rigor, and AI trust in one system.

The challenge

Disconnected systems create operational blind spots.

When HRMS, project delivery, time capture, payroll, finance, approvals, and executive reporting live in separate tools, every team optimizes locally while the enterprise loses visibility — revenue leaks quietly, payroll exceptions surface at lock, and leaders reconcile spreadsheets instead of running the business.

Scattered workflows across departments

Projects, people, sales, contracts, timesheets, and reports spread across separate systems slow teams down — every handoff loses context, and leadership spends Fridays reconciling status instead of acting on live operational intelligence.

Leadership blind spots at scale

Manual status reports replace real-time visibility into delivery health, workforce utilization, payroll readiness, and revenue exposure. Growing headcount and project volume outpace spreadsheet-based operations without a unified control tower.

AI and integration without governance

Bolting chatbots and connectors onto point solutions creates black-box automation and silent sync failures. Enterprise operators need persona-aware AI, universal approvals, and integration health — not another disconnected experiment.

What Tracopus delivers

  • One luxury operating layer for planning, execution, payroll, and executive review
  • Nine persona-aware experiences from employee self-service to CXO control tower
  • Universal approvals and audit-first governance across every domain
  • Ten AI agents with evidence, confidence, and human approval before production writes

One platform

PSA, portfolio, HRMS, payroll, and AI — unified.

Tracopus is a premium operating layer for services companies and enterprise delivery organizations: one shell, twelve modules, nine personas, ten AI agents, universal approvals, audit-first governance, and workflow-native intelligence without forcing teams into disconnected point solutions.

Connected Business Flow

From opportunity to insight — one continuous lifecycle.

Tracopus connects sales capture, contract clarity, staffed delivery, time and attendance, approvals, billing, payroll, and executive insight in one traceable flow — so every handoff preserves context instead of losing it in email and spreadsheets.

1

Capture the Opportunity

Sales teams track bid requests with account context, ownership, stages, expected value, documents, funnel analytics, and follow-up activity in a structured pipeline.

2

Win & Contract Clarity

Won opportunities convert to purchase orders or contract records — commercial terms, dates, and account linkage preserved for delivery and finance.

3

Launch Delivery with Context

Delivery spawns projects from contract context with AI-assisted WBS planning, member assignment, deliverables, and staffing agent recommendations.

4

Staff & Allocate Resources

Resource managers review capacity, skills, bench, roll-offs, and allocation timelines — balancing utilization, margin, and delivery commitments.

5

Execute Through Tasks & Governance

Teams run taskboards, Gantt, risks, issues, stage gates, and tickets while collaboration, files, and notes stay in project context.

6

Capture Time & Attendance

Employees submit timesheets and attendance; managers validate utilization; payroll readiness agents surface blockers before processing.

7

Approve, Bill & Pay

Universal approvals route leave, expenses, and timesheets; finance generates invoices from approved billable time; payroll runs publish paychecks with audit trail.

8

Review, Learn & Improve

Executive control tower, domain dashboards, AI briefings, lessons learned, and RCA patterns feed continuous improvement across the Work Graph.
