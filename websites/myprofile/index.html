Professional Overview

Aneeshkumar Perukilakattunirappel Sundareswaran is a senior technology professional with 18+ years of experience spanning full-stack application development, enterprise platform modernization, large-scale eCommerce systems, and cloud-native microservices architectures. His career reflects sustained leadership across design, development, migration, enhancement, support, and operations of mission-critical systems used by global enterprises.

He has deep expertise across the entire software development lifecycle, working within Agile, Rational Unified Process (RUP), Spiral, and structured Waterfall methodologies. In addition to advanced software engineering capabilities, Aneeshkumar brings strong people leadership and delivery management experience, including team planning, delegation, mentoring, performance management, conflict resolution, and long-term capability building.

Earlier in his career, he also gained professional experience in computer hardware maintenance and networking, which continues to inform his holistic understanding of systems—from infrastructure and runtime behavior to application-level performance and scalability.

He is an active contributor to the global technology community as a published technical author, senior and fellow member of multiple professional bodies, award winner, hackathon judge, and mentor, reflecting recognition beyond his day-to-day professional role.

Core Strengths

  • Translating complex business requirements into scalable, maintainable technical architectures
  • Collaborating effectively with senior stakeholders, enterprise architects, product owners, and delivery teams
  • Providing strategic, tactical, and operational guidance across distributed systems portfolios
  • Driving engineering excellence through code quality, testability, observability, and reliability
