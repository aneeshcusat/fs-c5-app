Tickets & Service

SLA-backed service desk — the team register My tickets slices — not project issues unless you keep them separate on purpose.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Work a service ticket to SLA.

Service desk

SLA tickets the requester already sees

Tickets & service is the team desk. My tickets is the same rows for the requester or assignee.

The job of this console

Work the queue to SLA, comment, resolve. Do not recapture the incident only in a project issue unless you mean RAID.

Seat at the table

PMs and agents. Requesters use My tickets. Admins see org-wide if scoped.

The working rhythm

Filter at-risk, open, act, confirm requester view.

Moments that bring you here

SLA breach warning. A production incident. After a workflow notify. When chat promised a ticket.

ⓘ
One ticket entity
Not a second helpdesk. Access/asset grants may still be Access requests.
