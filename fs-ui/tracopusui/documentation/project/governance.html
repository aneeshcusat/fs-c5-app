Steering pack

RAG assembled from RAID you already keep

Governance composes milestones, risks, and issues into a pack. It is not a third copy of the RAID log.

Projects → Governance Route /project/governance Org flag governanceEnabled Project Manager · Executive · Admin

The job of this console

Read the pack for steering, click through to fix red items, return. Do not maintain RAG only in slides.

Seat at the table

PMs, executives, admins. Contributors update tasks; they may not own the pack.

The working rhythm

Open pack, drill red, fix child register, refresh.

Moments that bring you here

Steering committee. Audit. After a red risk. Month-end delivery review.
