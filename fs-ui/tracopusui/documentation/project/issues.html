The job of this console

Log the issue, assign actions, close or promote to the service desk. Keep RAID honest.

Seat at the table

PMs and executives. Assignees complete actions. Service agents work tickets.

The working rhythm

Log, assign, chase due actions, close. Steering pack reads governance.

Moments that bring you here

Blocked environment. Failed deploy. Customer complaint that is still a project issue. After a risk crystallizes.

ⓘ
Issue ≠ ticket ≠ risk
Pick the entity that owns the work. Do not triple-enter the same story.
