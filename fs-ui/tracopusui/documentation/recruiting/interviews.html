The job of this console

Book interviewers, capture outcome, push the candidate forward or out. Keep the req attached.

Seat at the table

The working rhythm

Open upcoming, schedule, record outcome, return to candidates.

Moments that bring you here

On-site week. Panel conflict. A late reject after loop. Reschedule.
