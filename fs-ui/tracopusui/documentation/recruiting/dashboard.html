The job of this console

See aging and funnel gaps, then drill into the register that must move. Convert a hire into Employee before they appear on timesheet.

Seat at the table

HR Admin and Admin. Hiring managers may see scoped reqs on child screens. Candidates never see this tower.

The working rhythm

Open tower, pick a red funnel slice, act on that entity, return.

Moments that bring you here

Weekly hiring standup. A stalled req. Offer pending. After a role is filled and onboarding should start.

ⓘ
Candidate is not Employee
Workforce, leave, and pay start when Employee exists. This tower does not replace the directory.

Overview

Recruiting dashboard is a view of ATS entities. A hire must become Employee; the candidate record is not the workforce directory.

How to use

1. Do not staff from candidates
Capacity uses Employee.
2. Offers ≠ contracts
Purchase orders are commercial.
3. Drill to act
The tower does not edit offer letters in a chart.
