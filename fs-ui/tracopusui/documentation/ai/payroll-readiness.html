Gate hints

What still blocks the run — not the run itself

The job of this console

Read the gates, fix time or employee finance on those screens, then calculate. Never key a net pay into the agent.

Seat at the table

Payroll and finance with payrollReadinessEnabled. Employees do not clear org gates here.

The working rhythm

Before each cycle: hints, fix sources, run, publish.

Moments that bring you here

Two days before payday. A missing timesheet cluster. After a config change. When analytics shows a surprise.

ⓘ
No pay in the log
Masked summaries only. Bank, tax, and nets stay on payroll registers.
