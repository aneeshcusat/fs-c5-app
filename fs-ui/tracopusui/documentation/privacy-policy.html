Information we process

We process account details you provide (such as name, work email, and authentication credentials), workplace data your organization configures in Tracopus (for example projects, timesheets, leave, and attendance), and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

Information is used to authenticate users, deliver product features your organization enables, provide support, improve reliability and security, and meet legal or contractual obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with subprocessors that host or operate the service under contract, or when required by law. Your organization remains the controller of employee and workplace data entered into Tracopus.

Security and retention

We apply administrative and technical controls appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. Retention follows your organization’s configuration and applicable law.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact your organization administrator or Infleca Innovation Pvt Ltd through the support channels provided with your Tracopus subscription.
