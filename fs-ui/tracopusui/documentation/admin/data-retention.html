Data Retention

Retention schedules, purge windows, and compliance holds for operational records.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Set a retention schedule.

Keep, hold, purge

Schedules and legal holds on operational records

Data retention is the schedule of keep windows, purge eligibility, and compliance holds for operational records. A hold freezes purge; it does not freeze the live business record from legitimate edits.

Admin → Data retention Route /admin/data-retention Org flag dataRetentionEnabled HR Admin · Org Admin

The job of this console

Publish retention for each family, place a hold when legal asks, and understand what purge will touch before it runs. Coordinate with jobs that actually execute purge.

Seat at the table

Administrators with compliance duty. Employees never set their own retention. Developers do not add ad-hoc deletes that bypass these windows.

The working rhythm

Open a family, set the window, save, place or lift a hold, then confirm scheduled jobs. Spot-check audit after a purge window elapses.

Moments that bring you here

A litigation hold. GDPR-style deletion request that still must respect a hold. After a new module starts writing records with no schedule. Year-end policy review.

ⓘ
Holds beat purge
A compliance hold keeps records even when the calendar says they are eligible. Jobs honor this console; do not script around it.
