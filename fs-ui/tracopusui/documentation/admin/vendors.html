Admin → Vendors (deep link) Route /admin/vendors HR Admin · Org Admin · Finance operators with access

The job of this console

Keep the vendor master accurate, then use aggregates to see bids and purchase orders already linked. Create commercial documents on Sales / Finance, not as rows invented here.

Seat at the table

Administrators maintaining the master. Buyers work bids and POs on their finance screens. Employees do not maintain vendors.

The working rhythm

Search a vendor, confirm legal name and status, follow aggregates into bid or PO lists, correct the master if the name is wrong.

Moments that bring you here

A new supplier before the first PO. Duplicate vendor names breaking reports. After a Salesforce import of POs. When a vendor should be inactive but still appears on new bids.

ⓘ
PO and bid stay canonical
This register does not replace PurchaseOrder or BidRequest. Aggregates are counts and links, not a second contract store.
