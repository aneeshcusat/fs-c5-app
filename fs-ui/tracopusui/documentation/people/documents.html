People vault

Files that belong to the employee record

People → Documents Route /people/documents Org flag documentsEnabled HR Admin · Manager (scoped)

The job of this console

Find the person, store or retrieve a permitted file, and keep sensitive scans off tickets and graph snippets.

Seat at the table

HR and scoped managers. Employees may self-serve some categories during onboarding. Executives do not browse everyone’s IDs.

The working rhythm

Search employee, open vault, upload or download, close. Confirm retention on sensitive types.

Moments that bring you here

Contract renewal. ID expiry. Onboarding upload. A subject-access request.

ⓘ
Sensitive attachments
Do not persist raw bank, tax, or medical content in AI audit logs or Work Graph metadata.
