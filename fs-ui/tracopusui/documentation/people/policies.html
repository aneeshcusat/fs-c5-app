The job of this console

Publish a version, collect acknowledgements, see who is outstanding. Employees read and sign; they do not edit the text.

Seat at the table

HR authors. Managers and executives may see coverage. Employees acknowledge assigned policies.

The working rhythm

HR: draft, publish, watch acks. Employee: open, read, acknowledge.

Moments that bring you here

Annual handbook. A new code of conduct. Onboarding pack. Audit of who signed.

ⓘ
Not Global Policy Center
Leave, attendance, and timesheet rules are different products. This hub is handbook + ack.
