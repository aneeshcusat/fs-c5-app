The job of this console

Filter domain, open a card, follow the source, act there or mark the card unhelpful. Never copy unmasked pay or medical from a card into notes.

Seat at the table

Executives, managers, admins with aiInsightsEnabled and scope. Others must not see cross-team cards.

The working rhythm

Scan cards, open one source, decide, feedback, next card.

Moments that bring you here

Monday pack. After a control-tower red. When an agent suggested something you want to verify.

Overview

AI insights are composed cards with sourceEntityType and sourceEntityId. They are not business truth. Sensitive fields stay masked. Agent console is conversation; this page is the card register.

How to use

1. Always open the source
A confident card can still be wrong.
2. Cross-team scope
Cards outside persona should not appear.
3. Audit is separate
AI decision audit stores masked run metadata.
