Operational detail

Why a batch failed — not the employee file

Integration logs show connector errors and payloads. They are not Admin audit, not AI audit, and not a place to edit people or projects.

The job of this console

Find the failure, read the masked detail, fix mapping or the source, retry the job. Never paste raw payloads into chat or Work Graph.

Seat at the table

The working rhythm

From a red health card, open the log, fix, retry, confirm in product.

Moments that bring you here

A poison payload. Vendor schema change. Duplicate external ids. A compliance question about what was sent.

Overview

Integration logs are operational. Admin audit is the org event register. AI audit is masked AI runs. Logs must not become an unmasked warehouse of bank, tax, or medical payloads.

How to use

1. Retention
Follow data-retention policy.
2. PII in payloads
Treat as a mapping defect if raw sensitive fields appear.
3. Not a queue editor
You do not approve leave from a log line.
