Workbook coverage

Detailed workflow coverage

The supporting workbook sheets are summarized below in client-ready language.

Timesheet validation workflow

  • Available: validation, approval routing, detailed review, rejection, delegation, escalation, bulk action, and audit.
  • Client setup: confirm named project, account, business-unit, finance, and backup approvers.
  • Not yet available: Microsoft Teams approval actions and a dedicated approval-delay dashboard.

Missed submission and approval

  • Available: late classification from the server deadline preview, mandatory reasons, separate approval paths by lateness, reopening reasons, reminders, and audit.
  • Client setup: enable timesheet.reminders, timesheet.late-workflow, timesheet.period-lock, and timesheetSubmissionReminderEnabled; confirm the cut-off time, reminder days, approvers, locking schedule, and supported notification channels.
  • Not yet available: repeat-defaulter reporting and Microsoft Teams or WhatsApp actions.

Attendance and leave

  • Available: leave priority, office entry and exit, work-from-home hours, approved corrections, and limited overnight matching.
  • Expected protection: hours-only timesheets do not invent entry or exit times, and an unrecognized access card does not automatically mark absence.
  • Not yet available: automatic final attendance updates, attendance locking, a complete device feed, and dedicated exception reporting.

Validation readiness

Client validation preflight

Complete these checks before starting the requirement-by-requirement session.

1 · Business data

  1. Prepare active employees, projects, tasks, assignments, grades, skills, and leave records.
  2. Choose representative normal, late, rejected, approved, and locked timesheet periods.
  3. Agree expected hours, approvers, reminders, reports, and attendance outcomes.

2 · Rules and responsibilities

  1. Confirm the standard, moderately late, and significantly late approval paths.
  2. Confirm the cut-off, reminder, approval-deadline, locking, and reopening rules.
  3. Confirm every approver and backup approver is active and available for the test.

3 · Users and evidence

  1. Prepare employee, project lead, account lead, business-unit lead, finance, HR, and administrator test users.
  2. Confirm supported email, in-product, and mobile notification channels.
  3. Record screenshots, dates, sample names, expected results, actual results, and the final decision.

What each flag does when on or off

Catalog keys default off in code. On activates the behaviour in the On column. Off keeps the Off column. Dependent flags cannot turn on until their parent is on.

Open Admin → Feature flags only after HRMS.tracopusAdminAdditional.adminGovernanceEnabled and HRMS.tracopusAdminAdditional.featureFlagsAdminEnabled are on. If those two are off, the Feature flags screen is hidden and catalog keys cannot be changed from the UI.
