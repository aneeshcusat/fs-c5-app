NPD Ideas

Idea intake for new products — not a task backlog and not a bid request.

Intake

Ideas before there is a project

NPD ideas capture product thoughts. They are not Task, not Employee, and not a customer bid.

The job of this console

Write the idea, keep an owner, send it to gates. Create a Project only when the process says build.

Seat at the table

PMs and executives. Delivery contributors wait for a project.

The working rhythm

Capture, refine, submit to gate, park or promote.

Moments that bring you here

Hackathon follow-up. Customer wish that is not a bid. Strategy offsite. Duplicate idea merge.

ⓘ
Not a backlog
My work lists Task/TaskActivity. Ideas stay in NPD until delivery starts.
