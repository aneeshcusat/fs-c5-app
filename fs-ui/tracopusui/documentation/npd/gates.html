NPD Gates

Stage-gate decisions on NPD items — process decisions, not leave approval and not project governance RAG.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Pass or hold an NPD gate.

Stage decisions

Pass, hold, or kill — not a leave inbox

NPD gates are product-process decisions on ideas. They are not timesheet approval and not delivery governance RAG.

Projects → NPD gates Route /npd/gates Org flag npdEnabled Project Manager · Executive · Admin

The job of this console

Open the pending gate, review the packet, decide, and only then spawn a Project if the process says build.

Seat at the table

Executives and PMs. Employees do not vote here unless invited as reviewers.

The working rhythm

Queue of pending gates, decide, write the outcome on the NPD record.

Moments that bring you here

Stage-gate meeting. Funding hold. Kill a zombie idea. Pass to launch.

ⓘ
Different approval family
My Approvals is leave/time/access. Project governance is RAID. This is NPD process.
