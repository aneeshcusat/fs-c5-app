Learning Reports

Permission-scoped learning exports — snapshots of assignments and completions, not a warehouse you type into.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Export a learning completion report.

Learning export

Snapshots of who finished what

Learning reports export assignment and completion data you already have. The CSV is not a new LMS.

People → Learning reports Route /learning/reports Org flag learningEnabled Manager · HR Admin · Admin

The job of this console

Pick a report, filter, export. Correct overdue on assignments, then export again.

Seat at the table

Managers and HR with learningEnabled. Learners do not export org-wide files.

The working rhythm

Run, read, export if needed, fix sources, rerun.

Moments that bring you here

Audit pack. Quarterly board. After a compliance blast. A manager asking for team %.

ⓘ
Export is a snapshot
Persona filters apply. Do not load the CSV back as a learner master.
