The job of this console

Find overdue, nudge, or correct the assignment. Do not type completions into a side tracker.

Seat at the table

Managers and HR. Employees complete on My learning.

The working rhythm

Filter overdue, open a person, act, refresh.

Moments that bring you here

Audit week. A regulator request. After a blast assign. Before a role goes live.
