The job of this console

Open due items, finish the course, confirm complete. Ask HR if an assignment looks wrong.

Seat at the table

The working rhythm

Open queue, complete, stop when overdue is empty.

Moments that bring you here

Compliance deadline. New hire curriculum. A manager nudge. After a course update.

2. Due dates
Compliance views will flag overdue.
