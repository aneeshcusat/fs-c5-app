Bid Details

Full bid fields, study metadata, status workflow, linked POs — opened from list or View Bid Details sidebar.

Single bid view with study metadata, status transitions, and linked purchase orders.

Overview

/sales/bidrequest/:bidRequestId — comprehensive record for one bid request including study setup, screening, quota, and ownership.

Screen layout

Hero: Bid ID, status, owner, account, study type.
Body: Section cards for study basics, setup, screening, additional fields, quota.
Sidebar: Quick actions, linked POs, status history.

Header & filters

Status transitions via action buttons (New → Assigned → In progress → Won/Lost). No list-level SHOW/SORT on detail page.

Summary & charts

Detail may include inline statistics for this bid's stage history where configured.

Main content & actions

Update fields, change status, link/create purchase order from winning bid, open related projects.

Before you start

✅
Win checklist
Value confirmed · PO created/linked · delivery lead notified · documents uploaded.
⚠️
Stage regression
Moving backward may reset analytics — document reason in activity log.

Who uses this screen

Bid owners and sales managers editing a single opportunity — stage, value, documents, and PO linkage. Account managers review before executive approval.

ℹ️
Single bid workspace
Opened from Bid Requests row click — manage one opportunity end-to-end until Won/Lost.

Key concepts

Bid header

Title, stage badge, owner, account, estimated value, key dates, study metadata.

Stage progression

Buttons or dropdown to advance funnel — triggers activity log entry.

Documents tab

Proposals, SOW drafts, client attachments.

Activity log

Comments, stage changes, owner reassignments with timestamps.

Link to PO

Create new PO or associate existing contract when bid wins.

Common mistakes

⚠️
Advancing to Won without PO plan
Delivery team unprepared — create or link PO before or immediately on Won.
⚠️
Documents in email only
Attach to bid Documents tab — email threads are not searchable for audits.
⚠️
Stale value field
Forecast charts use header value — update after client negotiation.

FAQ

Can I reopen a Lost bid?

Org policy varies — may require manager approval and stage regression.

How do I create PO from here?

Link to PO action on Won bids — pre-fills account and value.

Who sees activity comments?

Bid team and managers with register access — not all employees.

Does stage sync to Salesforce?

When CRM integration enabled — check sync badge after changes.
