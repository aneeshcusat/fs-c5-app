Purchase Orders

Purchase order list representing contracts that may link to bids and spawn delivery projects.

Contract register with delivery funnel and 11 summary chart panels.

Overview

Sales → Purchase Orders (/sales/purchaseorders) tracks signed contracts from creation through completion and closure.

Screen layout

Header: Create, quick filters, SHOW, SORT, team, text filter, grid/list.
Summary: Delivery funnel + 11 chart panels.
Register: PO rows with status, account, delivery team.

Header & filters

Quick filters: All · Active · New · Created · In progress · Completed · Closed · Deleted · Archived
SHOW/SORT/team/filter/grid — same Lux list pattern as Bid Requests
Create — PO wizard

Summary & charts

The Lux summary strip at the top of this screen is collapsible. Expand it to reveal KPI tiles and chart panels; collapse it for a wider register. All charts respect your active filters (SHOW preset, team scope, date range, text search).

Contract portfolio analytics for the filtered PO register.

◆ Emerald Luxury UI theme · chart data refreshes when filters change

Who uses this screen

Sales managers, delivery leads, and finance tracking signed contracts — status, delivery health, and account concentration. Primary register after bids convert to POs.

Contract register

Common mistakes

⚠️
Standalone PO without account
Breaks invoice and project linkage — always select account.
⚠️
Ignoring overdue health slice
Small overdue slice compounds — review linked project dates weekly.
⚠️
Duplicate PO for same SOW
Check bid linkage donut — merge or archive duplicates.

FAQ

PO vs project?

PO = commercial contract; Project = delivery execution — one PO may spawn one or more projects.

Can PO exist without bid?

Yes — standalone for extensions; bid linkage donut tracks mix.

Who updates contract value?

Sales manager or finance on detail page — may need approval workflow.

How is overdue calculated?

Compare contract end/milestone dates to today for open statuses.
