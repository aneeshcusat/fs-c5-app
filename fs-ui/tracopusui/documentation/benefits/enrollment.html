The job of this console

Elect or assist elect, save, confirm the next run and carrier file will see it.

Seat at the table

Employees for self when enabled; HR/payroll for assist. Carriers do not enroll here.

The working rhythm

Open window, elect, save, spot-check payroll benefits.

Moments that bring you here

Open enrollment. New hire. Marriage/life event. A missed election before payday.
