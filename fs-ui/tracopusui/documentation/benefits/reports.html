The job of this console

Pick a report, export, fix elections if the count is wrong, export again.

Seat at the table

HR and payroll. Employees do not export org PHI.

The working rhythm

Run, read, export, fix source, rerun.

Moments that bring you here

Broker request. Audit. After open enrollment. Finance asking headcount on a plan.
