The job of this console

Author and publish plans so enrollment has something to point at. Keep medical detail out of graph metadata.

Seat at the table

HR and payroll admins. Employees do not edit the catalog.

The working rhythm

Create, publish, tell enrollment, confirm a test election.

Moments that bring you here

Annual renewal. New carrier. A plan retirement. Before open enrollment.

ⓘ
Catalog, not PHI store
Do not persist medical or bank values in AI/graph. Carrier files are the exchange.
