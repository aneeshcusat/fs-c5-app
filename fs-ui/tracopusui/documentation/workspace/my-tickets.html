The job of this console

Track what you opened, answer a waiting-on-you prompt, and close when the desk agrees. Raise new work on the team tickets screen if you have it.

Seat at the table

Employees and assignees with myTicketsEnabled. Service owners use Projects → Tickets & service.

The working rhythm

Filter mine, open, comment, save. Watch SLA on the ticket detail, not on this list alone.

Moments that bring you here

A laptop request that became a ticket. Waiting on you. After an SLA reminder. When chat said ‘I’ll ticket it’.

ⓘ
One ticket entity

Overview

My tickets is a personal slice of the ticket / service desk entity. It is not a second helpdesk. Access requests for laptops still live under Admin access requests when that is the domain.

How to use

1. Same ticket everywhere
Do not recapture the issue in chat as the only copy.
2. SLA lives on the ticket
This queue does not override policy.
3. Sensitive data
No passwords or payroll files in comments.
