Workspace Home

Persona-aware home with shortcuts to approvals, work, and notifications — a launch pad, not a second work tracker.

Launch pad

Shortcuts to work you already own

Home greets you with persona-aware doors into My work, approvals, and notifications. It does not keep a second list of tasks, pay, or tickets.

The job of this console

Orient for the day, then jump. Read what is waiting, open the owning screen, and leave Home behind until the next landing.

Seat at the table

Every signed-in person with homeEnabled. Cards you cannot open stay hidden by persona navigation and flags — not by deleting the route.

The working rhythm

Land, scan shortcuts, click one destination, act there. Return only when you need a different door.

Moments that bring you here

First screen after login. After a role switch. When you cannot remember whether something lives under People or Projects.

ⓘ
Home is not a source
Tasks stay on Task / TaskActivity. Approvals stay on domain requests. Paychecks stay on payroll publishes.

Overview

Workspace Home is a persona-aware launch pad over Employee, Task, TaskActivity, and approval inboxes you already have. Cards are shortcuts, not a shadow backlog.

How to use

1. Treat cards as doors
Click through; do not expect to complete a timesheet on Home.
2. Persona changes the map
A manager sees approvals; an employee may not.
3. Favorites are personal
They do not change org navigation policy.
