Performance Reviews

Write-ups on Employee for a cycle — ratings and narrative, masked, not the compensation award itself.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Complete a performance review.

The write-up

Ratings on the person, pay on payroll

Performance reviews are the cycle write-up on Employee. They are not salary structures and not a public feed.

The job of this console

Open your packet, write against goals, submit. Managers complete team packets. HR watches the cycle.

Seat at the table

Employees, managers, HR as the cycle defines. Executives may see calibration, not every narrative.

The working rhythm

Open, write, submit, wait for calibration if any.

Moments that bring you here

Self-review due. Manager write-up. After 1:1s. Before calibration.
