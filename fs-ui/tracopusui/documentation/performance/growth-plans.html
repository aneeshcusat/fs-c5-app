Growth Plans

Development plans on Employee — learning and career actions, not the LMS catalog and not a PIP unless you model it that way.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Create a growth plan and assign learning.

Development

A plan on the person, courses on the LMS

Growth plans record development intent on Employee. Completing training still goes through learning assignments and My learning.

The job of this console

Write the plan, assign real courses, check in on 1:1s. Do not use it as a pay letter.

Seat at the table

Employees and managers. HR may see org development. Payroll does not calculate from this.

The working rhythm

Create, link learning, review in 1:1s.

Moments that bring you here

After a review. Promotion track. Skill gap from the matrix. New role.

ⓘ
Intent, then LMS
Courses catalog is learning. This plan is talent. Mask sensitive career notes.
