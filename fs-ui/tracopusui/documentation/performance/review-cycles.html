Review Cycles

Windows that open performance reviews — cycle admin, not the review write-up itself.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Open a review cycle.

The window

When reviews are open

Review cycles define the window and population. The write-up lives on Performance reviews.

The job of this console

Create the cycle, set dates, launch, close when done. Do not type ratings into the cycle row.

Seat at the table

HR admins. Managers write on reviews. Employees participate when included.

The working rhythm

Create, launch, monitor dashboard, close.

Moments that bring you here

Annual kickoff. A delayed cycle. Population miss. After calibration.

ⓘ
Window, not the rating
Reviews hold the content. Compensation is a later payroll step.
