Submit, conflict, lock — the manager pass

Timesheet approval is the manager pass: submitted weeks (or day batches), conflict resolution, and payroll-period lock. It sits on the same timesheet records the grid wrote.

People → Timesheets (approval) Route /people/timesheet Manager · HR Admin · Payroll (lock window)

The job of this console

Approve or reject a week, resolve overlapping hours, and understand when lock freezes the period. Do not re-key hours here unless the product offers a correction path.

Seat at the table

Managers of people who submit. Employees submit from the grid. Payroll cares about lock, not individual cells.

The working rhythm

Open the approval queue, filter the week, open conflicts, approve or reject, then lock when the org’s payroll calendar says so.

Moments that bring you here

Monday after submit. Two projects claiming the same afternoon. Payroll cutoff. A missing leave row that should have filled hours.

Overview

Timesheet approval adds submit, manager review, conflict resolution, and payroll lock on top of the weekly grid. Delegation may allow acting approvers. When Work-Time late-workflow is on, late weeks can require a late reason on submit and may route through published late bands (TIMESHEET-LATE-LTE7 / TIMESHEET-LATE-GT7) instead of the standard chain.

Partial-week flag (timesheetAdditional.partialWeekSubmissionEnabled, default off):

  • Off — employee submits the full week; one pending week; week locked until approve/reject; inbox id WEEK:{id}.
  • On — employee selects days (chips) and may submit multiple batches per week; each batch is a separate approval request (ts_timesheet_submission_model); leftover DRAFT/REJECTED days stay editable; approve/reject by submission; inbox id SUBMISSION:{id}. Fully APPROVED weeks still require reopen before edit.

How to use

1. Employee submits
Full week when partial is off; select day chips and submit a batch when partial is on. If Work-Time late-workflow is on and the week is past cut-off, the employee must complete a late reason first.
2. Manager reviews
Pending panel or Approvals inbox (week or submission card). Late weeks may arrive on the moderately late chain (TIMESHEET-LATE-LTE7, business-unit) or the significantly late chain (TIMESHEET-LATE-GT7, business-unit then finance) when those policies are published.
3. Resolve conflicts
Clear overlaps before approve (scoped to the pending week or batch). Late reason is audit context — the employee does not pick the approval route.
4. Approve or reject
Approve to complete the step, or reject with a comment so the employee can fix and resubmit.
5. Lock when ready
Payroll / role month lock still applies. With timesheet.period-lock on and lockAfterDays set (often 7), approved weeks also auto-lock via TIMESHEET_PERIOD_LOCK; reopen may require a reason.
