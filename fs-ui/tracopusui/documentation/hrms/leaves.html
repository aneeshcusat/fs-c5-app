Leave Management

Apply for leave, track balances, submit for approval, and see manager decisions — with working-week and holiday aware day counts.

Scroll to Flowcharts for animated step-by-step diagrams. Admin setup: Leave Configuration.

1 — End-to-end leave

The full path from “policy ready” to timesheet impact. Use this when explaining leave to a new team.

flowchart TD
  A[Policy and balances ready] --> B[Apply]
  B --> C[Submit]
  C --> D[Approve or Reject]
  D --> E[Balance + timesheet]
  1. 1
    Policy and balances readyPublished leave policy (or classic) plus opening balance or accrual so the employee has available days.
  2. 2
    ApplyEmployee opens Leave Management, picks type and dates; day count uses their working week and holidays.
  3. 3
    SubmitOverlap and working-day checks run; pending balance is reserved; approver is notified.
  4. 4
    Approve or RejectManager or delegate decides. Reject requires a comment and releases pending balance.
  5. 5
    Balance + timesheetOn approve: debit ledger, promote leave TaskActivities, optional attendance sync.

2 — Request lifecycle statuses

How a leave row moves through workflow statuses, including reject and withdraw paths.

flowchart TD
  C[CREATED] --> S[SUBMITTED]
  S --> A[APPROVED]
  A --> P[Promoted / Synced]
  S --> R[REJECTED]
  S --> W[Withdraw / Cancel]
  W --> Rel[Pending released]
  R --> Rel
  1. 1
    CREATEDDraft leave after create; not yet in the manager queue.
  2. 2
    SUBMITTEDAwaiting approval; balance held as pending.
  3. 3
    APPROVEDAuthority accepted; debit starts; promotion may still be pending/retrying.
  4. 4
    Promoted / SyncedTimesheet leave activities exist; attendance may show LEAVE when sync is on.
  5. 5
    REJECTEDManager declines with comment; pending balance released.
  6. 6
    Withdraw / CancelEmployee or HR stops the request per status rules.
  7. 7
    Pending releasedHeld days return to available after reject/withdraw.

3 — Employee apply leave

What the employee does in the Apply leave drawer, including fixing overlap or non-working ranges.

flowchart TD
  O[Open Leaves] --> B[Check balance]
  B --> A[Apply leave]
  A --> T[Pick type and dates]
  T --> D[Review day count]
  D --> V{Overlap or non-working only?}
  V -->|Yes| Fix[Fix dates]
  Fix --> T
  V -->|No| Sub[Submit for approval]
  1. 1
    Open LeavesHRMS → Leaves (or People → Leaves) with leave module enabled.
  2. 2
    Check balanceConfirm available days for the leave type on the balance cards.
  3. 3
    Apply leaveOpen the apply drawer from the header action.
  4. 4
    Pick type and datesChoose leave type, from/to, optional half-day and reason.
  5. 5
    Review day countCount excludes weekly offs and holidays for this employee.
  6. 6
    Overlap / non-working checkSystem blocks bad ranges; fix dates if needed.
  7. 7
    Fix datesAdjust range until overlap and working-day rules pass.
  8. 8
    Submit for approvalRequest becomes SUBMITTED and appears in the manager queue.

4 — Manager approve / reject

Manager or delegate decision path from pending panel or Approvals inbox.

flowchart TD
  P[Pending panel or Approvals inbox] --> Rev[Review dates and days]
  Rev --> Dec{Decision}
  Dec -->|Approve| Deb[Debit balance]
  Deb --> Pro[Promote TaskActivity]
  Pro --> Att[Attendance sync if flagged]
  Dec -->|Reject| Com[Reject + required comment]
  Com --> Rel[Pending balance released]
  1. 1
    Open pendingLeave Management pending panel or Workspace Approvals inbox.
  2. 2
    ReviewCheck employee, type, dates, day count, and reason.
  3. 3
    DecisionApprove or reject (reject needs a comment).
  4. 4
    Debit balanceApproved leave moves days from pending to used.
  5. 5
    Promote TaskActivityLeave days appear on timesheet; retries if promotion fails.
  6. 6
    Attendance syncWhen flagged, writes LEAVE / HALF_DAY on the attendance matrix.
  7. 7
    Reject + commentEmployee is notified; request closed as REJECTED.
  8. 8
    Pending releasedHeld balance returns to available.

5 — How day count is resolved

Why Saturday may count (Mon–Sat) or Friday may not (Sun–Thu). Priority: employee override → jurisdiction → team → fallback.

flowchart TD
  Emp[Leave subject employeeId] --> Res[Working-day resolver]
  Res --> P1{Override?}
  P1 -->|Yes| WD[Workdays set]
  P1 -->|No| P2{Jurisdiction rule?}
  P2 -->|Yes| WD
  P2 -->|No| P3[Team / schedule / Mon-Fri]
  P3 --> WD
  WD --> Hol[Minus holidays]
  Hol --> Days[numberOfDays]
  1. 1
    Leave subjectResolver uses the employee the leave is for — not only the team.
  2. 2
    Working-day resolverSingle service used by create, submit, promote, and the apply drawer.
  3. 3
    Employee override?Personal working-day rule wins if assigned.
  4. 4
    Workdays setISO weekdays that count (e.g. 1–5 or 7,1–4 for Sun–Thu).
  5. 5
    Jurisdiction rule?Country/region pattern if the employee is assigned a leave jurisdiction.
  6. 6
    Team / schedule / Mon–FriTeam-default rule, then work schedule/shift, then Mon–Fri.
  7. 7
    Minus holidaysPeople holidays matching assigned calendar keys are excluded.
  8. 8
    numberOfDaysServer recalculates on create/submit; drawer estimate is guidance only.

Overview

HRMS → Leaves is the employee and manager hub for time off. When leaveManagementEnabled is on, you create and submit requests here; managers approve from the pending panel or Approvals inbox.
