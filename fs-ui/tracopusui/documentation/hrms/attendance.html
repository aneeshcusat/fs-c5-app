Overview

HRMS → Attendance is the month-at-a-glance control surface for who was present, absent, on leave, or needs correction. Managers mark cells, approve pending items, and (when enabled) process regularization. Approved leave can auto-write attendance when leaveAutoSyncToAttendance is on.

Screen layout

  • Header — team scope, employee filter, Documentation link, month navigation via summary.
  • Summary strip — attendance rate and absence KPIs for the filtered month.
  • Pending approvals — attendance marks / regularizations awaiting decision.
  • Matrix — employees × days; color-coded cells; click to mark or view details.
  • Current-user panel — your own pattern when viewing self.
  • Payroll lock banner — soft lock when prior-month edits are frozen (no matrix export).

Flags & prerequisites

  • Role: hrmsModules.attendanceEnabled + attendance CRUD as needed.
  • Org: attendanceAdditional.attendanceRegularizationEnabled for regularization flows.
  • Org: leaveAdditional.leaveAutoSyncToAttendance for leave → attendance writes.
  • Optional: shift policies (/admin/shift-policies), holidays (Leave Configuration → Holidays), delegation for approvers away.
