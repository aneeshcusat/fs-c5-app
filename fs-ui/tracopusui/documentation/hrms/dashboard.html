Employee Dashboard

Two-tab dashboard: My dashboard for individuals and Team dashboard for managers with org-wide workforce analytics.

Personal and team analytics — attendance, utilization, task mix, and workforce demographics for managers.

Overview

HRMS → Dashboard is the default landing for many HRMS users. Individual contributors see personal utilization; managers with team-switch permission see a second tab with headcount, churn, and demographic charts.

Screen layout

Top: Page header with module kicker and tab bar (My dashboard | Team dashboard).
Body: Responsive grid of chart panels and lists — each panel is a card with title, chart, and optional drill action.
Team tab only: Team scope bar above analytics to filter organizational unit.

Header & filters

Tabs: My dashboard · Team dashboard (managers only).
Team scope bar (Team tab): select organizational unit to narrow all downstream charts.
No SHOW/SORT on dashboard — scope is tab + team selector only.

Summary & charts

The Lux summary strip at the top of this screen is collapsible. Expand it to reveal KPI tiles and chart panels; collapse it for a wider register. All charts respect your active filters (SHOW preset, team scope, date range, text search).

Switch tabs to change the entire panel set.

◆ Emerald Luxury UI theme · chart data refreshes when filters change

Expand the summary strip, scan utilization donut, then open Timesheet to fill gaps for the current week.
💡
Manager handoff
Before 1:1s, set Team scope to the report's org unit and screenshot trend charts for discussion.

Who uses this screen

Every employee for the My dashboard tab; people managers and HR leads for the Team dashboard tab with org-wide workforce analytics. Open this screen at the start of the week to review utilization, attendance, and task load.

ℹ️
Two tabs, two audiences
Individual contributors should stay on My dashboard. Switch to Team dashboard only if your role includes direct or org-unit reports — the Team tab exposes headcount and demographic charts.

Key concepts

My dashboard

Personal attendance strip, six-week hour stack, weekly utilization donut, task queue mix, and upcoming leave/holidays.

Team dashboard

Manager-only analytics: headcount trends, grade distribution, gender and tenure mix, department bars, and team-scoped filters.

Summary strip

Collapsible KPI row above tabs; expand for charts, collapse for a cleaner view. State persists in browser storage.

Utilization

Billable hours divided by available hours — target varies by org policy (often 75–85% for client-facing roles).

Team scope bar

On Team tab, filters every chart to selected organizational units — always set scope before interpreting numbers.

Common mistakes

⚠️
Using Team tab without scope
Managers view blended company-wide data and draw wrong conclusions — always narrow the team scope bar first.
⚠️
Ignoring non-billable hours
A healthy billable ratio still requires tracking internal and training time; zero non-billable entries often indicate under-reporting.
⚠️
Checking dashboard once a month
Utilization and task mix are weekly signals — review Monday or after timesheet submission deadlines.

FAQ

Why can't I see the Team tab?

Your account lacks manager permissions or has no direct/indirect reports in Tracopus org structure.

Do dashboard charts respect filters?

Team tab charts follow team scope; My tab is always personal data regardless of global header search.

Can I export dashboard data?

Export lives on underlying registers (Timesheet, Employees) — dashboard is read-only analytics.

What utilization target should I aim for?

Org-specific — check with your delivery lead. The donut shows actuals; policy targets may appear as reference markers.
