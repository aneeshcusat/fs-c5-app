Invoices

Invoice list with status chips, FY scope, segment bar, monthly billing bar, and outstanding panel.

Billing register with fiscal-year analytics and status filtering.

Overview

HRMS → Invoice tracks billing documents from draft through paid/closed with fiscal-year scoped analytics.

Screen layout

Header: Create, status chips, FY selector, SHOW/SORT, filter text.
Summary: Status segment bar, monthly billing bar, outstanding panel (non-Apex).
Register: Invoice rows with status, amount, account, dates.

Header & filters

Status chips: All · Draft · Pending · Approved · Paid · Closed
FY scope: Current financial year or selectable historical years
SHOW/SORT/filter — standard list controls
Create — sidebar to new invoice

Summary & charts

The Lux summary strip at the top of this screen is collapsible. Expand it to reveal KPI tiles and chart panels; collapse it for a wider register. All charts respect your active filters (SHOW preset, team scope, date range, text search).

◆ Emerald Luxury UI theme · chart data refreshes when filters change
  • Status segment bar — proportional colored bar with legend for invoice status mix
  • Monthly billing bar — invoiced amount trend by month within FY
  • Outstanding panel — open amounts awaiting payment

Main content & actions

Create via sidebar; click row for Invoice Details. Document layout route available for PDF/share.

Before you start

ℹ️
Billing cycle
Align invoice period with timesheet week lock — most orgs bill one week after period end.
📝
Multi-currency
Currency display follows account settings on the linked client record.

Who uses this screen

Finance staff, billing coordinators, and account managers tracking client invoices generated from billable work. Project managers may have read-only access to reconcile hours before send.

ℹ️
Billing register hub
Start here to find draft invoices, track outstanding amounts, and open detail pages for PDF send — not for logging hours (use Timesheet).

Key concepts

Invoice register

Sortable list of invoices with status, client, period, amount, and reference numbers.

Status workflow

Draft → sent → paid (and void/cancelled) — transitions happen on detail page.

Summary KPIs

Outstanding, paid, and draft totals in collapsible summary strip.

SHOW / SORT / Filter

Status presets, column sort, text search on client or invoice number.

Timesheet traceability

Lines on detail page link back to logged billable hours where configured.

Permissions

RoleAccess on this screen
Finance / billingFull create, edit, send, mark paid on invoices.
Account managerView invoices for assigned accounts; may create drafts.
Project managerRead-only on projects they lead — verify hours before finance sends.
Individual contributorUsually no access unless dual finance role.
📝
Administrator override
Org administrators and service accounts configured in the Admin console may bypass row-level scopes shown above. When in doubt, confirm access with your Tracopus administrator.

Common mistakes

⚠️
Sending draft with unapproved timesheets
Billable pool may change — lock or approve timesheets for the billing period first.
⚠️
Wrong billing period
Period drives which hours aggregate — off-by-one week errors are common at month boundaries.
⚠️
Ignoring PO reference
Client AP teams reject invoices without PO number — copy from Sales purchase order.

FAQ

Where do invoice amounts come from?

Primarily billable timesheet hours × rates, plus manual adjustments on detail page.

Can I invoice without a project?

Manual line items allowed for some orgs — default is project-linked billable pool.

Who can mark an invoice paid?

Finance role typically — updates status and KPI tiles.

How do I regenerate PDF?

Open invoice detail → PDF preview/download after line changes.
