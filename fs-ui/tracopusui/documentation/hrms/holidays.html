Public and company days

The calendar leave counting actually uses

Holidays maintains public and company holidays by location so leave day counts and calendars stay accurate. A missing holiday is how a ‘working Friday’ gets billed as leave.

People → Holidays Route /people/holidays Org flag holidaysEnabled HR Admin · Admin

The job of this console

Keep location calendars true: add a public holiday, add a company shutdown, retire a date that was wrong. Legal calendars and working-week rules still live under Leave admin; this is the holiday list those engines read.

Seat at the table

HR administrators. Employees see holidays on leave and timesheet UIs; they do not edit the list.

The working rhythm

Pick a location, add or edit a date, save, then preview a leave request that spans it. Import via legal calendars when the org uses operator-assisted import.

Moments that bring you here

Diwali / Thanksgiving week. A new location. A gazetted holiday announced late. After leave counts looked a day high.

ⓘ
Holidays feed leave and attendance
Do not maintain a parallel holiday spreadsheet. Legal calendar import still lands here for the dates people see.

Overview

People → Holidays is the source calendar for non-working public days. Leave Configuration maps jurisdiction → calendar key so day counts stay correct across regions.

How to use

1. Create holidays for the year
Add national and regional days with consistent location keys.
2. Map in Policy Center
Assign calendar keys under Calendars in Global Leave Policy Center.
3. Assign employees
Ensure employees have jurisdiction (or working-day overrides) as needed.
4. Spot-check leave apply
Apply leave across a holiday and confirm the day is excluded.
