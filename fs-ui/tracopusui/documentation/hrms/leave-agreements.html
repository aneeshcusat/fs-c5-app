Overview

Agreements and contract overrides encode special leave deals without forking the whole policy catalog. Screens are primarily registers; create/update via forms when available, or API. Always keep clear effective dates and audit reasons on writes.

How to use

1. Open Agreements
Admin → Leave → Agreements / Contract overrides.
2. Review register rows
Inspect active agreements; create/update via forms when available, or API.
3. Check effective windows
Confirm start/end on any seeded or updated row.
4. Verify on leave balances
Confirm overrides appear on Leave Management when resolve applies them.
