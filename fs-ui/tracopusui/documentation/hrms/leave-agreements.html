Contract overlays

Agreements and overrides on top of published policy

Leave agreements hold contract overrides — the exceptions a named assignment needs without forking the global policy. Create/update via forms when available, or API.

Admin → Leave agreements Route /admin/leave/agreements HR Admin · Org Admin

The job of this console

Record an overlay for a person or contract, keep the global policy clean, and retire the override when the assignment ends.

Seat at the table

HR administrators. Employees see the effect on balances and apply, not this register.

The working rhythm

Find the agreement, edit override, save, prove with a sample apply or test lab. Remove when the contract ends.

Moments that bring you here

An offer letter with extra privilege days. A union overlay. Someone moving off a contract. When global policy was cloned ten times instead of overridden.

ⓘ
Override, not a new policy family
Published policy remains the base. Agreements are exceptions, not a second Global Policy Center.

Overview

Agreements and contract overrides encode special leave deals without forking the whole policy catalog. Screens are primarily registers; create/update via forms when available, or API. Always keep clear effective dates and audit reasons on writes.

How to use

1. Open Agreements
Admin → Leave → Agreements / Contract overrides.
2. Review register rows
Inspect active agreements; create/update via forms when available, or API.
3. Check effective windows
Confirm start/end on any seeded or updated row.
4. Verify on leave balances
Confirm overrides appear on Leave Management when resolve applies them.
