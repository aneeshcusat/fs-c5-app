Billing Plans

How a contract will be billed over time — plans on PurchaseOrder / project, not the invoice register itself.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Attach a billing plan to a contract.

Bill schedule

When the contract turns into invoices

Billing plans schedule invoices against a PO or project. The invoice list remains the bill register.

Finance → Billing plans Route /finance/billing-plans Org flag billingPlansEnabled Finance Manager · Admin

The job of this console

Attach a plan, keep it aligned with the contract, and let invoices flow — do not type the same amount twice.

Seat at the table

Finance managers and admins. Delivery PMs see project financials, not always this editor.

The working rhythm

Open plan, attach PO/project, set dates, confirm invoice drafts.

Moments that bring you here

Milestone billing. Monthly retainers. A contract change. Before first invoice.

ⓘ
Schedule, not the bill
Invoice is the document. Credit notes adjust bills. Recognition is a sibling.

Overview

Billing plans describe when to bill. Invoices are the documents. Recognition may follow a related policy.

How to use

1. One plan per contract intent
Do not fork silently.
2. Invoices win as bills
Plan is the schedule.
3. Change control
Altering a live plan mid-invoice needs care.
