Finance Dashboard

Finance control tower over POs, invoices, and project financials — charts on live commercial entities, not a warehouse you type into.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Scan finance health and drill a red KPI.

Commercial tower

Money views of POs, invoices, and projects

The finance dashboard rolls up live commercial records. It is not a general ledger you journal into.

Finance → Finance dashboard Route /finance/dashboard Org flag dashboardEnabled Finance Manager · Executive · Admin

The job of this console

See what is off, drill into PO, invoice, or project financials, fix there, return.

Seat at the table

Finance managers, executives, admins. PMs use project financials. Payroll uses payroll dashboards.

The working rhythm

Scan, drill, correct source, refresh.

Moments that bring you here

Month-end. A leakage spike. Unbilled delivery. After a big PO.

ⓘ
Composed, not posted
PurchaseOrder is the contract. Invoices bill. This tower does not replace either.
