Write-offs

Uncollectible amounts — explicit write-off documents, not a silent edit of invoices or leakage charts.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Write off an uncollectible balance.

Uncollectible

Give up on a balance on purpose

Write-offs are explicit documents that a balance will not be collected. They are not a hidden chart edit.

Finance → Write-offs Route /finance/write-offs Org flag writeOffsEnabled Finance Manager · Admin

The job of this console

Select the amount, record a reason, post. Confirm invoices/AR and leakage follow.

Seat at the table

Finance managers and admins. Executives may approve.

The working rhythm

Identify uncollectible, post write-off, confirm related views.

Moments that bring you here

Bad debt. Customer gone. After legal settle. Leakage you will not bill.

Own document
Not a credit note. Not a PO change. Audit wants this register.
