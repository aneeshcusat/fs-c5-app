The job of this console

Keep methods true, generate period entries, approve, close. Fix the contract if the schedule is nonsense.

Seat at the table

Finance managers and admins. Executives read forecast/analytics, not always this hub.

The working rhythm

Policies → schedules → entries → period close, then dashboards follow.

Moments that bring you here

Month close. Milestone method change. Audit of recognized revenue. After a credit note.

ⓘ
Entries on commercial sources
PO and invoices remain canonical. This hub posts recognition, it does not invent contracts.
