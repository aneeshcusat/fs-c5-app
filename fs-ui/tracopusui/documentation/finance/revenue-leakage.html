Revenue Leakage

Unbilled, under-billed, or leaked value vs delivery and contract — a detective view, not a write-off register.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Investigate an unbilled leakage row.

Detective view

Where value leaked vs the contract

Revenue leakage highlights unbilled or under-billed delivery against POs. It does not post a journal.

Finance → Revenue leakage Route /finance/revenue-leakage Org flag revenueLeakageEnabled Finance Manager · Executive · Admin

The job of this console

Find a gap, open the project or PO, then invoice, fix time, or write off on those consoles.

Seat at the table

Finance and executives. PMs may be asked to explain delivery, not to post.

The working rhythm

Filter gaps, drill, act on invoice/write-off/timesheet, refresh.

Moments that bring you here

Month-end unbilled. A forgotten milestone bill. After lock. Board asking about leakage.

ⓘ
Finder, not a ledger
Invoices bill. Write-offs forgive. Credit notes reverse. This screen points.
