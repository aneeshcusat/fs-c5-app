Expenses (Finance View)

Finance operations view of employee expense claims — the same expense records employees submit, not a second petty-cash book.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Review an employee expense as finance.

Claims ops

Employee expenses, finance seat

This is the finance view of the same claims employees submit. It is not vendor AP and not a petty-cash sidecar.

Finance → Expenses (finance view) Route /finance/expenses Org flag expensesEnabled Finance Manager · Admin

The job of this console

Review, approve or bounce, send toward reimbursement. Confirm the employee still sees the same claim.

Seat at the table

Finance managers. Employees use Payroll → Expenses (employee). Payroll may pay via reimbursements.

The working rhythm

Filter pending, open, decide, watch pay path.

Moments that bring you here

Month-end claims. A duplicate receipt. Policy breach. After travel week.

ⓘ
One expense entity
Two menus, one record. Vendor invoices remain supplier bills.
