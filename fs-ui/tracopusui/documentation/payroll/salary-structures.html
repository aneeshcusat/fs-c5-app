Salary Structures

Earnings and deduction components that compose employee pay — assigned to Employees, not offer letters as the live structure.

Pay shape

Components that a run will calculate

Salary structures define how an Employee is paid. Offers are ATS. Runs calculate. Mask the numbers.

The job of this console

Keep structures accurate, assign them to people, then calculate on a run.

Seat at the table

Payroll managers. HR may request changes. Employees see net on slips, not this editor.

The working rhythm

Edit structure, assign, prove on a draft run.

Moments that bring you here

New hire mapping. Annual increment. A new earning code. After an offer accept.

On Employee
