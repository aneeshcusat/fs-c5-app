Bench & Roll-off

Availability and roll-offs on Employee — the ready pool for staffing, not a punishment list and not candidates.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Place someone from bench onto a request.

Ready pool

Who is free next — from real assignments ending

Bench & roll-off shows Employee availability as projects end. It is supply for staffing, not a performance ranking.

The job of this console

See who is coming free, match to a request, place on Team capacity, watch the bench row clear.

Seat at the table

Resource and project managers. Employees may see their own end date on My work, not this board as authors.

The working rhythm

Scan roll-offs, open a request, place, confirm utilization.

Moments that bring you here

End of a PO. A gap next month. After a cancellation. Staffing standup.

ⓘ
Derived from assignments
Not candidates. Not a second Employee list. Performance is a different module.
