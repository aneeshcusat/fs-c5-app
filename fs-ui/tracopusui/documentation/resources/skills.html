Proficiency, certifications, and gaps on Employee — then staff from capacity and bench, not a shadow talent table.

Who can do it

Skills on Employee, staffing on capacity

The skills matrix maps proficiency on people you already employ. Placing them on work still happens on Team capacity or staffing requests.

The job of this console

Find a skill gap or a match, open the Employee, then assign on the live planner.

Seat at the table

Resource managers, HR, PMs. Employees may see their own skills on profile if offered.

The working rhythm

Filter skill, pick a person, jump to capacity or a request.

Moments that bring you here

A niche skill on a bid. Certification expiry. After training completes. Bench with unused skills.
