Staffing Requests

Demand tickets to place people from skills and bench onto projects — fulfilled as assignments, not as a second project team table.

Scroll to Flowcharts. Scenario: Raise and fulfill a staffing request.

Demand tickets

Ask for people, place them on the live roster

Staffing requests capture project demand. Filling them writes assignments on Team capacity — not a parallel team list.

The job of this console

Raise or triage demand, match from skills and bench, place, close the request.

Seat at the table

PMs raise; resource managers fulfill. Employees appear as supply, not authors.

The working rhythm

Open queue, match, place on capacity, close.

Moments that bring you here

New project from a PO. Sudden absence. Skill gap. After simulation looks feasible.

ⓘ
Demand, then assignment
ATS requisitions hire people. This queue places Employees you already have.

Overview

Staffing requests are demand. Fulfillment writes Team capacity assignments on Employee × Project. ATS requisitions are hiring demand.

How to use

1. Project must exist
Do not staff a bid-only wish without a project if policy requires it.
2. Close the request
Or the queue lies.
3. AI staffing agent
Suggests; you still commit on capacity.
