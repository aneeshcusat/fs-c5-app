Timesheet & Record

Record time with week navigation, day entry forms, and access-controlled date validation.

Weekly timesheet and day-level record screens for billable, non-billable, and leave entries.

Overview

Timesheet on mobile mirrors web policy: users record weekly hours through day-level forms with strict date/access checks.

Screen layout

Weekly screen: Week navigation, summary metrics, day rows.
Record screen: Billable, non-billable, leave sections with dynamic dropdowns.

Header & filters

Days outside access policy are disabled. Hours format accepts H or H.MM (minutes in MM).

Summary & charts

Summary header shows recorded and category totals aligned with Insights metrics for the same week.

Main content & actions

Open timesheet via FAB from any hub; select day row to add or edit entries.
