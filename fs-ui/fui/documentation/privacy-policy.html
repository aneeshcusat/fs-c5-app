Privacy Policy - FamStack Mobile

Last updated: 2026-04-16

This Privacy Policy explains how FamStack Mobile ("the App", "we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Android application.

📌 Note: This page can be used as the publicly accessible Privacy Policy URL required by Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only information required to provide app functionality and secure authentication.

  • Account identifiers (such as email ID and user ID) used for device registration and login
  • Device identifier generated for secure device-based authentication
  • Authentication token issued by backend services after successful login
  • Timesheet and work entry information that you create in the app
  • Basic technical data required for app operation and error handling

2. Camera Permission

The App requests the android.permission.CAMERA permission for QR code scanning.

  • Purpose: to scan registration/login QR codes generated from the web application
  • Camera access is used only when you actively open the scanner feature
  • We do not continuously access the camera in the background
  • We do not use camera access for advertising or profiling

3. How We Use Information

  • Register your mobile device securely
  • Authenticate your account
  • Enable PIN/biometric-based sign-in workflows
  • Display assigned tasks/projects and allow timesheet submission
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized access

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Sensitive data such as auth/session details are stored using secure device storage where supported
  • Data transmission between app and backend services is performed over network calls configured by your organization
  • Access to account data is controlled through authenticated sessions and organizational permissions

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Information may be shared only with:

  • Your organization's backend systems that operate FamStack services
  • Service providers required to run the app infrastructure (if applicable under your organization's setup)
  • Legal/regulatory authorities when required by law

6. Data Retention

Data is retained according to your organization's policies and backend system configuration. Locally stored authentication/session data may remain on the device until you sign out, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

7. Your Choices

  • Revoke camera permission from device settings (QR login/registration may stop working)
  • Request data-related actions through your organization administrator
  • Stop using the app and uninstall it at any time

8. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for business/organizational use and is not directed to children under 13.

9. International Data Processing

Depending on your organization infrastructure, data may be processed in regions where your backend services are hosted.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact your organization administrator or support team.
