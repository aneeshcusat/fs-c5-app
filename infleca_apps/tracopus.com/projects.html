The challenge

What breaks when work lives in disconnected tools.

Point solutions optimize one department while the enterprise loses traceability — approvals stall, payroll exceptions surface late, revenue leaks between delivery and finance, and leaders reconcile exports instead of drilling from insight to source record.

Governance gaps outside the project record

Risks, issues, stage gates, and decisions tracked in spreadsheets never connect to the project timeline, billing readiness, or portfolio roll-up. PMOs lose audit trail when governance lives outside the delivery system of record.

No portfolio control tower

Executives lack a single view connecting delivery health, resource utilization, financial exposure, risk heatmaps, and approval bottlenecks. Weekly status decks replace live operational intelligence that should drill to source records.

Ticket and service silos

Service work disconnected from project context, SLAs, timesheets, and billing readiness means revenue leakage and client dissatisfaction surface late. Tickets must live in the same Work Graph as deliverables and invoices.

Lessons learned never feed forward

Root causes and lessons learned captured in retrospectives never flow into WBS planning, risk registers, or staffing decisions for the next engagement. Knowledge dies in slide decks instead of improving the next project kickoff.
