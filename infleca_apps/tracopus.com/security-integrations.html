Enterprise control without sacrificing experience.

Persona-aware access, universal approvals, field masking, audit-first mutations, and connector governance — Tracopus gives IT and compliance teams the controls they need while employees and managers keep focused, luxury workspaces.

Identity without persona scope

SSO alone is not enough — employees still see admin registers, executives still see payroll detail, and export policies vary by screen instead of by role. Persona context must resolve scope, masking, navigation, and AI behavior from a single model.

Connectors without observability

Salesforce, Jira, Entra ID, and payroll providers sync silently until something breaks. Without sync logs, retry queues, field mappings, and trust scores, IT discovers stale data from user tickets instead of health dashboards.

Approvals and audit fragmented

Leave, timesheets, expenses, documents, assets, project gates, budgets, and invoices each need the same approval engine — delegation, SLA tracking, evidence, and audit events — not separate inboxes per module.

What Tracopus delivers

  • Microsoft Entra ID SSO on web and mobile with MFA on employee profiles
  • Universal approval engine with delegation and full audit trail
  • Connector catalog with sync health, retry queues, and developer marketplace
  • Field masking and export controls aligned to persona and permission

Connector strategy

Plug systems into the Tracopus operating model.

Identity providers, CRM, dev tools, payroll systems, and HR platforms sync through governed adapters — with field mappings, sync logs, retry queues, trust scores, and health dashboards so IT and operations teams see integration status before users report stale data.
