The challenge

What breaks when work lives in disconnected tools.

Point solutions optimize one department while the enterprise loses traceability — approvals stall, payroll exceptions surface late, revenue leaks between delivery and finance, and leaders reconcile exports instead of drilling from insight to source record.

Fragmented employee records

HR data scattered across spreadsheets, shared folders, and a separate payroll system means no single view of attendance today, leave balance, timesheet compliance, or payroll readiness. Managers and HR chase updates instead of acting on live Employee 360 intelligence.

Late payroll exceptions

Attendance gaps, pending leave approvals, and unapproved timesheet weeks surface only at payroll lock — forcing emergency corrections, manual overrides, and delayed paychecks. Without a Payroll Readiness Score, every run starts with detective work.

Document and policy compliance gaps

Policy acknowledgments, document expiry, and verification status tracked outside the system create audit risk. HR cannot see which employees are blocked on onboarding checklists or which contracts expire before the next review cycle.

Offboarding and asset risk

Assets, IT access, final timesheets, and payroll blockers missed during employee exit create security exposure and settlement delays. Checklist-driven offboarding with audit trail is essential for regulated and enterprise operations.

FAQ

Questions for this module

How does Employee 360 work?

Employee 360 combines profile, employment, attendance, leave, timesheets, payroll preview, projects, documents, assets, approvals, and activity in one operational view.

Does Tracopus process payroll?

Yes — salary structures, payroll runs, exceptions, paychecks, expenses, reimbursements, and benefits are built into the People and Payroll modules with readiness agents and universal approvals.

Can managers edit locked timesheet weeks?

Only when role grants allow — past-week locks and edit windows are set in Application Configuration.
