About Neetu Mohanan

Neetu Mohanan is an aspiring elementary educator based in Tracy, California, originally from Kerala, India. She is completing her credential through Residency@TCSJ (Residency at Teachers College of San Joaquin), a teacher preparation program focused on developing reflective, equity-minded practitioners for Central Valley classrooms.

Education & professional background

Neetu earned a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from Mahatma Gandhi University, Kerala (2013), and completed a Post Graduate Program in Business Analysis and Business Intelligence through the University of Texas McCombs School of Business and Great Learning (2020). She is pursuing an MCA in Artificial Intelligence at Amrita University (expected 2026).

Before entering teacher preparation, Neetu worked as a Java Developer at Infleca Innovation Pvt Ltd (2019–2021), where she built Spring Boot applications and collaborated on database-driven solutions. That analytical background informs her commitment to clear instruction, step-by-step learning, and high expectations for every student.

Teaching identity & Who Am I? presentation

Neetu’s e-portfolio includes The Quiet Girl Who Dreamed to Shine, her Who Am I? presentation shared with her Residency@TCSJ cohort. The narrative explores Kerala roots, quiet strength, cross-cultural identity, and her journey from mathematics and software development to elementary education—aligned with authenticity, connection, and uniqueness criteria.

Philosophy, equity & classroom practice

Neetu believes learning begins with relationships. Her philosophy emphasizes rigorous learning, genuine belonging, and noticing both student voice and silence—especially for quiet learners like she once was. Her equity beliefs center on access, honoring the whole child, and creating varied ways for students to participate and grow.

Her portfolio documents coursework in culturally responsive teaching (Culturally Responsive Teaching and the Brain by Zaretta Hammond), English learner strategies, classroom culture routines, weekly reflections (Learned · Felt · Wondering), and tools for inclusive elementary practice.

Residency@TCSJ program

Teachers College of San Joaquin (TCSJ) prepares educators for California classrooms through integrated coursework, fieldwork, and performance assessments. The Residency@TCSJ e-portfolio documents meaningful artifacts across five phases—from preparing for classroom placements through becoming a reflective practitioner.
