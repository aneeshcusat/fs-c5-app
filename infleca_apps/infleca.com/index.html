FAQ

Questions we hear often

Quick answers about how we work with clients, what to expect in an engagement, and how to get started. For anything specific to your organization, use Talk to us and we will respond with next steps.

  • What types of projects does Infleca take on?

    We build and evolve enterprise web and mobile applications, cloud platforms, AI systems, integrations, and our own product suite for operations, projects, knowledge, learning, and intelligent workflows. Engagements range from governed AI platform delivery to multi-phase modernization programs.

  • Can you work alongside our in-house engineering team?

    Yes. Many clients keep core ownership in-house while we accelerate delivery, harden architecture, or own specific workstreams. We align on branching, code review, release cadence, and documentation so collaboration stays predictable.

  • How do you approach security, privacy, and compliance?

    We design with least-privilege access, secure transport and storage patterns, and clear audit trails where operations require it. We can adapt to your policies and review checkpoints; for regulated industries we recommend early alignment on data classification and deployment constraints.

  • Which regions and time zones do you support?

    We collaborate with clients globally from our corporate office in San Francisco, USA and our delivery hub in Cochin, Kerala. We schedule overlap for stand-ups, demos, and stakeholder reviews, and we document decisions so distributed teams stay aligned.

  • What is the best way to start a conversation?

    Use Talk to us on this page with a short summary of your goals, timeline, and constraints. We typically reply within one to two business days with clarifying questions or a suggested discovery call.
